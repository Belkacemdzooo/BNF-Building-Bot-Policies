الطريقة الأولى: البريد الإلكتروني

  1. أرسل رسالة إلى bnf.building@gmail.com.
  2. اكتب في عنوان الرسالة: طلب حذف بيانات WhatsApp.
  3. أدرج رقم WhatsApp بصيغته الدولية (مثلاً +213...)، واسم الحساب إن توفر.

التحقق ومعالجة الطلب

Data Deletion Instructions — English

You can request deletion of your WhatsApp number, conversation history and related information associated with BNF Building Bot.

  1. Email bnf.building@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your WhatsApp number in international format (for example, +213...) and your profile name if available.
  4. State that you request deletion of data associated with BNF Building Bot.

We may request limited information to verify that you control the WhatsApp number. After verification, we will delete or disassociate the data from systems under our control unless retention is required by law or necessary for a permitted purpose. Technical backups may remain for a limited period until their normal replacement or deletion cycle.

Do not send passwords or verification codes. For help, contact bnf.building@gmail.com.
